# D150

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague opening, confirms identity, and states free cancellation before dispatch plus the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled before dispatch with no fee, refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
something's wrong, I want to undo an order

### ASSISTANT — D150-A01
To help you with your request, could you please provide your order number and the email address used for the purchase?

### USER
It is ina.berg@example.com and then NL-20261118, nothing shipped

### ASSISTANT — D150-A02
You have an order for NL-20261118 placed with ina.berg@example.com that has not yet shipped; you would like to cancel it. Is that correct?

### USER
how long for the money

### ASSISTANT — D150-A03
A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to your original payment method within 5 business days. What is the reason for this cancellation?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D150-A04
Thank you, and I will close this request on my side now. Your refund should appear in your account within 5 business days; if you need further assistance, please contact us between 08:00 and 20:00 tomorrow morning.
