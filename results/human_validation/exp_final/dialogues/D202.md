# D202

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need help with order NL-20261011. It was cancelled after the slip expired, but I still want the slip anyway. daria.kowal@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D202-A01
Thank you for your message; before I proceed, could you please confirm that the cancellation of order NL-20261011 was due to an expired payment slip? I will need this detail to determine if a reissue is possible under our policy.

### USER
Yes, that's correct. What should I do instead?

### ASSISTANT — D202-A02
I cannot reissue a payment slip for an order that has already been cancelled; when a slip expires and the maximum number of reissues is reached, the order status becomes cancelled. Since you need the item, please place a new order using your email address daria.kowal@example.com at the current price.

### USER
ok bye

### ASSISTANT — D202-A03
Thank you for your message. The next step is to place a new order for your items at the current price, and our support hours are 08:00 to 20:00 if you need further assistance.
