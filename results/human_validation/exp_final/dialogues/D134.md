# D134

## Success criterion

The agent says an exchange for another size depends on stock, and when unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit valid for 12 months.

## Reference answer

An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit. Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F23 — An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit.
- F24 — Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Please exchange the blue jacket from NL-20260630 for a larger size. hana.weiss@example.com

Thank you, Hana. To confirm your request regarding order NL-20260630 purchased with hana.weiss@example.com: would you like an exchange for a larger size or a refund? Please note that if the new size is unavailable, you may choose between a refund or store credit.

### ASSISTANT — D134-A02
Yes, if the larger size is unavailable, you may choose between a refund or store credit. Store credit remains valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.
