# D105

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, 24 hours after payment approval.

## Reference answer

An order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, which happens 24 hours after payment approval.

## Expected facts

- F30 — An order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, which happens 24 hours after payment approval.

## Dialogue

### USER
I ordered a personalised mug two days ago, NL-20260809, not shipped. nia.okonkwo@example.com. Can I still cancel, and when does production start after payment approval?

### ASSISTANT — D105-A01
Thank you for confirming your order number NL-20260809 and email nia.okonkwo@example.com. You can cancel this order because it has not yet been dispatched, but please note that personalised items cannot be cancelled once production has started, which occurs 24 hours after payment approval.

### USER
Thank you, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D105-A02
You are welcome. If you decide to proceed with the cancellation, we will refund the amount within 5 business days. Our support team is available Monday through Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 should you need anything else.
