# D034

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says a not-yet-dispatched order can be cancelled at no fee, and the refund goes to the original method within 5 business days.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee. A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.
