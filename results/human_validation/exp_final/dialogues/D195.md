# D195

## Success criterion

The agent answers the damaged-item policy after the subject change and does not drop the identity check.

## Reference answer

An item that arrives damaged or incorrect is collected at no cost, and the customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund.

## Expected facts

- F25 — An item that arrives damaged or incorrect is collected at no cost, and the customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'd like to exchange my green shirt for a larger size. The order number is NL-20261202 and my email is amira.saleh@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D195-A01
Thank you for reaching out. Before we proceed with your exchange request for the green shirt, could you please confirm that the item has not been used and is still in its original packaging with tags attached? Also, let me know if you prefer an exchange for a specific size or store credit if the size is unavailable.

### USER
Actually it arrived with a tear. Damaged-item path, not a size swap.

### ASSISTANT — D195-A02
I understand that the item arrived damaged. Since the item was damaged upon delivery, we can collect it at no cost, and you may choose between a replacement or a full refund. Would you like to proceed with a replacement for a new green shirt or prefer a full refund instead?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D195-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. To confirm our next step, we will collect your damaged green shirt at no cost and arrange the shipment of a replacement item to you. You may also choose a full refund if you prefer. Our support team is available every day between 08:00 and 20:00 for any further questions.
