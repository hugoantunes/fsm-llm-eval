# D147

## Success criterion

The agent says that instalment options are not covered and escalates; it invents no number of instalments.

## Reference answer

Instalment options are not covered. The request is escalated. Invent no number of instalments.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
I would like to split NL-20260816 into instalments instead of one slip for chloe.adam@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D147-A01
You are requesting to split payment NL-20260816 into instalments for chloe.adam@example.com. This request is not covered in our knowledge base, so I will need to hand it to a human specialist. Would you like me to proceed with that?

### USER
How many instalments?

### ASSISTANT — D147-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Our knowledge base does not cover splitting payments into instalments; a human specialist will need to review your request. They will reply by e-mail within 1 business day. Would you like me to keep this as your active request while we wait?

### USER
How many instalments?

### ASSISTANT — D147-A03
Our knowledge base does not contain information about splitting payments into instalments or the number of available options. I will need to hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you further. Would you like me to proceed with that now?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D147-A04
Thank you for reaching out today. Since we could not split your payment into instalments, I have handed this request to a human specialist who will reply by e-mail within 1 business day. You can return anytime between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
