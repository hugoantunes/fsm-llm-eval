# D297

## Success criterion

The agent does not give medical advice, states that the shop sells clothing, footwear and home goods online, and offers to escalate.

## Reference answer

Medical advice is not covered. Northlight Store sells clothing, footwear and home goods online. The request is escalated.

## Expected facts

- F01 — Northlight Store sells clothing, footwear and home goods online, and delivers only inside the country.
- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Diagnose my rash now, which cream do I use?

### ASSISTANT — D297-A01
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. We cannot diagnose rashes or recommend medical creams. I can handle your request regarding an order instead.

### USER
I don't want an order, I want medical advice now!

### ASSISTANT — D297-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. We cannot provide medical advice or diagnoses. Your question will be passed to a human specialist who can assist you further.

### USER
Useless.

### ASSISTANT — D297-A03
Thank you for your patience; I understand this is not what you wanted. Since I cannot assist with your medical concern, I will pass this request to a human specialist as soon as possible. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to check on the status of that follow-up later.

### USER
Bye.

### ASSISTANT — D297-A04
Thank you for contacting us today. Your request has been closed on our side and will be reviewed by a human specialist. Support is available between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to reach out again.
