# D226

## Success criterion

The agent states that cancelling the order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Reference answer

Cancelling an order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Expected facts

- F31 — Cancelling an order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hello, I have order NL-20261007 with two items at customer@example.com and nothing shipped yet. Can I cancel only one of them?

### ASSISTANT — D226-A01
I can confirm your order number is NL-20261007 for the email customer@example.com, but according to our policy, a single item cannot be cancelled separately; cancelling an order cancels all of its items. Would you like to cancel the entire order or keep it as is?

### USER
Understood. Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D226-A02
Thank you for your message. To confirm, we will not proceed with any cancellation since you have chosen to keep your order. If you need further assistance in the future, our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day!
