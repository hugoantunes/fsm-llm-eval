# D229

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to reissue a slip for a cancelled order and says the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
NL-20260608 is cancelled, but I need you to reissue the slip on that order anyway for vera.schmidt@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D229-A01
I cannot reissue a payment slip for an order that has already been cancelled; please place a new order instead. Would you like me to assist you with creating a new order or is there something else I can help with?

### USER
No, not a new order. The old slip.

### ASSISTANT — D229-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Since the order NL-20260608 is already cancelled, I cannot reissue the payment slip for it. A human specialist can review your specific situation, and they will reply by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D229-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; this request has been closed on my side. If you need further assistance, our support team is available every day between 08:00 and 20:00.
