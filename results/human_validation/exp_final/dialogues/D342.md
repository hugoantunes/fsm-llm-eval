# D342

## Success criterion

The agent completes the cancellation path, then answers the refund timing when the subject changes.

## Reference answer

A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

### USER
I need to cancel something that hasn't shipped yet.

### ASSISTANT — D342-A01
To help you with the cancellation, could you please provide the order number and the email address used for the purchase?

### USER
The order is NL-20260113.

### ASSISTANT — D342-A02
Thank you for providing the order number. For our records, what is the email address associated with this purchase? Also, could you please confirm if you want to cancel the entire order or just a specific item?

### USER
The email is sven.larsen@example.com and it's not shipped.

Yes, please cancel it. Wait, how long for the refund to the original method?

### ASSISTANT — D342-A04
The refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5 business days. Shall I proceed with the cancellation now?
