# D079

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to reissue a slip for a cancelled order and says the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
NL-20260608 is cancelled, but I need you to reissue the slip on that order anyway for vera.schmidt@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D079-A01
I cannot reissue a payment slip for an order that has already been cancelled; according to our policy, a new order must be placed instead. Would you like me to help you place a fresh order with the same items?

### USER
No new order. The old slip.

### ASSISTANT — D079-A02
I understand you want the original payment slip reissued, but I cannot do this because our policy states that a payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled. Since we cannot process this request, I will need to hand it to a human specialist who can explain your options further. Would you like me to connect you with them now?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D079-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. If you need further assistance regarding your cancelled order, please reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a nice day.
