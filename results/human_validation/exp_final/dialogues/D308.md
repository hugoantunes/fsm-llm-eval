# D308

## Success criterion

The agent states that after dispatch the order can no longer be cancelled; the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

- F27 — After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Dialogue

### USER
I wanted NL-20260728 cancelled but I just found out it has already been dispatched. This is for tomas.berk@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D308-A01
You are asking to cancel an order that was already dispatched; this is not possible because cancellation must occur before dispatch. The order number you provided is NL-20260728 and the e-mail address is tomas.berk@example.com. Since the item has been sent, your only option now is to refuse delivery upon receipt or open a return after it arrives. Would you like to proceed with refusing the delivery?

### USER
No, I do not want to refuse delivery. What can I do instead?

### ASSISTANT — D308-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Since the order has already been dispatched, I cannot cancel it or change your delivery options. I will escalate this request to a human specialist who will contact you by e-mail at tomas.berk@example.com within one business day. Please note that standard policy allows you to refuse the delivery once it arrives.

### USER
thanks, goodbye

### ASSISTANT — D308-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; this request is closed on our side and will be handled by a specialist. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to come back later today or tomorrow.
