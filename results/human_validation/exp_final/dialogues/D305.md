# D305

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague opening, confirms identity, and states free cancellation before dispatch plus the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled before dispatch with no fee, refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
something's wrong, I want to undo an order

### ASSISTANT — D305-A01
I can help with that, but first I need to confirm your order number and the email address used for the purchase. Could you also tell me which item this is about and why you'd like to cancel or return it?

### USER
it was sent to ina.berg@example.com and then NL-20261118, nothing shipped

### ASSISTANT — D305-A02
Thank you, I have noted the email address as ina.berg@example.com and the order number as NL-20261118. Could you please tell me which item in this order you would like to cancel and the reason for the cancellation?

### USER
how long for the money

### ASSISTANT — D305-A03
Once we process the cancellation, the refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5 business days. Would you like to proceed with cancelling the entire order now?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D305-A04
Thank you for reaching out. If you change your mind or need further assistance, our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00.
