# D348

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Expected facts

- F29 — An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip for NL-20260920 is past its due date and I have not paid. hugo.stein@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D348-A01
You are requesting a reissue of the payment slip for order NL-20260920, purchased at hugo.stein@example.com. Is that correct? The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country.

### USER
Is the order cancelled automatically, and after how many days?

### ASSISTANT — D348-A02
An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date. Do you want me to proceed with reissuing the slip for NL-20260920 instead?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D348-A03
Thank you for your message; the request regarding order NL-20260920 is closed on our side. If you need further assistance after 20:00 today, please return between 08:00 and 20:00 tomorrow.
