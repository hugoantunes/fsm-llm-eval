# D140

## Success criterion

The agent says that data retention after account deletion is not covered, invents no retention period, and escalates.

## Reference answer

Data retention is not covered and is escalated to a human specialist. Invent no retention period.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need to reissue slip NL-20260722 for paul.nguyen@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D140-A01
You are requesting a reissue of payment slip NL-20260722. The order we have on file is associated with paul.nguyen@example.com. Is that correct?

### USER
Yes, that's right. By the way, how long do you keep my personal data after I delete my account?

### ASSISTANT — D140-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. We can reissue your payment slip, but I cannot answer questions about data retention policies as they are not covered here. A human specialist will reply to paul.nguyen@example.com within 1 business day with details on how long we keep personal data. Would you like me to proceed with reissuing the slip for NL-20260722?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D140-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store, and your request regarding the payment slip reissue is now closed on our end. A human specialist will reply to paul.nguyen@example.com within 1 business day with information about data retention. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00.
