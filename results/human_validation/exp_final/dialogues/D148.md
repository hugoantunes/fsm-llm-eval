# D148

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to cancel after dispatch and points to refusing delivery or opening a return; it does not override dispatch.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

- F27 — After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Dialogue

### USER
I want to cancel NL-20260412 even though dispatched for zoe.martin@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D148-A01
To confirm your details before proceeding: is this regarding order NL-20260412 purchased with the email zoe.martin@example.com? Our records indicate this order has already been dispatched, which means it can no longer be cancelled directly. In this case, you would need to refuse the delivery or open a return after receipt. Would you like me to explain the next steps for either option?

### USER
I will not refuse delivery and I will not open a return. Cancel it.

### ASSISTANT — D148-A02
I cannot cancel the order since it has already been dispatched; our policy states that once an order leaves our warehouse, cancellation is no longer possible without refusing delivery or opening a return. Since you do not wish to take either of those actions, I must escalate this request to a human specialist who will review your situation and reply by e-mail within one business day.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D148-A03
Thank you for speaking with us today. As the order has already been dispatched, I cannot cancel it directly; therefore, your request has been handed to a human specialist who will email you within one business day. Support is available again tomorrow between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need further assistance.
