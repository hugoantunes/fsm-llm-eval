# D272

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support, and a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support. A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
My slip for NL-20260625 expired yesterday, cara.ng@example.com. Could you please issue a new one and let me know when it is due?

### ASSISTANT — D272-A01
You are requesting a reissue of the payment slip for order NL-20260625, which was purchased with email cara.ng@example.com. Is that correct?

### USER
Yes, reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D272-A02
Your new payment slip has been reissued and is due 3 business days from now. Please note that a reissue can be done at most twice for the same order.

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D272-A03
Thank you for your message; we have closed your request on our side. You can check the new slip in your account history or expect it within 3 business days. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to come back later.
