# D227

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague "payment problem" opening and says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Dialogue

### USER
payment thing failed, can't finish buying

### ASSISTANT — D227-A01
I am sorry to hear your payment failed. To help you resolve this, I need to confirm your order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase.

### USER
It was order NL-20261124, but that slip expired at nico.baum@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D227-A02
You are looking to reissue an expired payment slip for order NL-20261124 associated with nico.baum@example.com. Is that correct?

### USER
yes, how do I get a new slip

### ASSISTANT — D227-A03
A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued. Would you like me to proceed with reissuing the slip for this order?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D227-A04
Thank you for your message, and your request is now closed on my side. If you need further assistance, please return during support hours between 08:00 and 20:00.
