# D259

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague opening, confirms identity, and states free cancellation before dispatch plus the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled before dispatch with no fee, refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
something's wrong, I want to undo an order

### ASSISTANT — D259-A01
I can help with that, but first please confirm your order number (format NL-XXXXXXXX) and the email address used for the purchase. Also, has the order already been dispatched?

### USER
ina.berg@example.com and then NL-20261118, nothing shipped

### ASSISTANT — D259-A02
Thank you, I have noted your order number NL-20261118 and email ina.berg@example.com. Since the order has not yet been dispatched, it can be cancelled with no fee. Could you please tell me which item from this order you wish to cancel?

### USER
how long for the money

### ASSISTANT — D259-A03
The refund will be processed back to your original payment method within 5 business days after the cancellation is confirmed. Do you still want to proceed with cancelling the entire order, or would you prefer to keep it and return a specific item instead?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D259-A04
Thank you for contacting us. The next step is that your order will be cancelled upon confirmation, and the refund will appear within 5 business days. You may reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
