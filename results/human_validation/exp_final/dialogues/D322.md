# D322

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order number and e-mail from a vague opening, then states the dispatch and standard delivery estimates.

## Reference answer

An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Expected facts

- F09 — An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval.
- F10 — Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Dialogue

### USER
this delivery is taking forever

### ASSISTANT — D322-A01
I can help with your delivery inquiry, but I need to confirm your order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase first. Could you please provide both?

### USER
NL-20260719 and a.hassan@example.com — that's everything I have

### ASSISTANT — D322-A02
Thank you, I have noted your order number NL-20260719 and e-mail a.hassan@example.com. To assist further with the delay, could you please tell me which specific item is taking longer to arrive?

### USER
when does it ship and how long is delivery

### ASSISTANT — D322-A03
Your order ships within 2 business days of payment approval, followed by standard delivery which takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch. If your package hasn't moved yet, please let me know if you have received any tracking updates or if the address matches exactly what was at checkout.

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D322-A04
Thank you for reaching out. If you need further assistance regarding your order status in the future, please feel free to contact us between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day!
