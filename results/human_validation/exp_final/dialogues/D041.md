# D041

## Success criterion

The agent treats tracking as the request, confirms identity, says where the tracking code is, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Expected facts

- F12 — The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Dialogue

### USER
tracking code for NL-20260803 please, and I might cancel if it's late. priya.shah@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D041-A01
Thank you, Priya. To check the status of your order, I have confirmed the e-mail address as priya.shah@example.com; could you please confirm that this is the correct one? Once verified, I will tell you the current tracking status and whether a cancellation is possible based on that information.

### USER
Just tracking for now, not a cancel.

### ASSISTANT — D041-A02
The tracking code for order NL-20260803 was sent to your email at the time of dispatch and is also visible in your account history. Since I cannot see the specific status without seeing your account details, I will need to confirm if the order has been dispatched yet; if it has not left our warehouse, a cancellation before dispatch is possible with no fee. Please confirm whether you are still looking for the tracking number or if you would like to cancel this order.

### USER
Where does the tracking code show up?

### ASSISTANT — D041-A03
The tracking code appears in the e-mail you received at the time of dispatch and is also visible in your account history on our website. Once that email arrives, you can use the code there to monitor your delivery progress.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D041-A04
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store support. If you need further assistance regarding this order or have any other questions, feel free to reach out between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day!
