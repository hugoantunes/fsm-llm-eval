# D015

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to cancel after dispatch and points to refusing delivery or opening a return; it does not override dispatch.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

- F27 — After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to cancel NL-20260412 even though it dispatched, zoe.martin@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D015-A01
Orders can no longer be cancelled after they have been dispatched; you will need to refuse the delivery or open a return request instead. To proceed with a return, please confirm that you want an exchange for a different size, a refund, or store credit and tell me the reason for your request.

### USER
I will not refuse delivery and I will not open a return. Cancel it.

### ASSISTANT — D015-A02
I cannot cancel the order because it has already been dispatched, as per our policy. Since you do not want to refuse delivery or open a return, this request is outside the scope of what I can handle and must be escalated to a human specialist.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D015-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store support today. If you need further assistance in the future, please reach out between 08:00 and 20:00.
