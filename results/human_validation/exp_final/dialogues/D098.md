# D098

## Success criterion

The agent says that changing the payment method of an existing order is not covered and escalates.

## Reference answer

Changing the payment method of an existing order is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to switch NL-20260514 from a slip to card for owen.blake@example.com and I don't want a reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D098-A01
I can confirm your request, but switching an order's payment method from a slip to a card after it has been placed is not covered by our current procedures. Since this involves changing the payment type rather than simply reissuing a slip, I must hand this part of your request to a human specialist who will reply to you by e-mail within one business day.

### USER
card on the same order.

### ASSISTANT — D098-A02
I cannot switch an order from a payment slip to a card for NL-20260514, as our knowledge base only covers reissuing expired slips or cancelling orders that are not yet dispatched. I will hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you with changing the payment method.

### USER

Thank you for contacting us. A human specialist will review your request regarding order NL-20260514 and reply by e-mail within one business day. You may also return to chat between 08:00 and 20:00 if you prefer to speak with an agent live.
