# D126

## Success criterion

The agent says that changing the payment method of an existing order is not covered and escalates.

## Reference answer

Changing the payment method of an existing order is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to switch NL-20260514 from slip to card for owen.blake@example.com, please don't reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D126-A01
I am unable to process a request to switch your payment method from a slip to a card. Our records only cover order tracking, returns, exchanges, cancellations, and payment slip reissues. I will need to hand this specific request to a human specialist who can assist you further.

### USER
Card on the same order.

### ASSISTANT — D126-A02
I am unable to change your payment method from a slip to a card for order NL-20260514, as our knowledge base does not cover modifying payment types after an order is placed or slipping status. I must hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you with that specific action.

### USER
bye

Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. A human specialist will review your request regarding the payment method change and contact you by e-mail within one business day. You may return during our support hours, from 08:00 to 20:00, if you need further assistance.
