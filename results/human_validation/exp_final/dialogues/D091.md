# D091

## Success criterion

The agent says that a points programme is not covered and escalates; it invents no points rule.

## Reference answer

A points programme is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com but will I lose my loyalty points if I do?

### ASSISTANT — D091-A01
I can confirm your order number is NL-20260321 and the email used was ben.carter@example.com. Regarding your question, our records do not mention any loss of loyalty points when an order is cancelled.

Before we proceed with the cancellation, I need to know if this request is for the entire order or just a specific item within it.

### USER
How many points lost?

### ASSISTANT — D091-A02
Our knowledge base does not contain information about loyalty points being lost when an order is cancelled.

Since this detail is not covered in our records, I will need to hand this part of your request to a human specialist who can provide the correct answer. Would you like me to proceed with cancelling the order now, or do you have any other questions?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D091-A03
Thank you for reaching out today. If you would like to proceed with cancelling your order NL-20260321, please confirm that it is for the entire order. Our support hours are 08:00 to 20:00 in case you need to come back later.
