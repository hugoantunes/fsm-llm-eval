# D040

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, states the 30-day window, the unused and tagged condition, and the 10-business-day refund after inspection.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, unused, with tags attached and in its original packaging. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F19 — A returned item must be unused, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.
