# D131

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D131-A01
I understand your request concerns the reissue of a payment slip for order NL-20260706 purchased by luis.ferreira@example.com. Our policy allows at most two reissues per order, so I cannot issue a third one and must escalate this to a human specialist.

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D131-A02
After the second reissued slip expires, the order is cancelled. If you have an active order awaiting payment, please confirm if you would like to place a new order instead.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D131-A03
Thank you, and that request has been closed on our side. You can call us back between 08:00 and 20:00 for further assistance.
