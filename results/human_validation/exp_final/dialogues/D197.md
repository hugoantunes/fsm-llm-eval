# D197

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Expected facts

- F29 — An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip for NL-20260920 is past its due date and I have not paid. hugo.stein@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D197-A01
Thank you for providing the details. Since your order number is NL-20260920, I see that this payment slip has expired. According to our policy, we can reissue an expired payment slip up to two times per order; however, a reissued slip becomes due 3 business days after it is issued. Would you like me to proceed with requesting a new payment slip for you?

### USER
Is the order cancelled automatically, and after how many days?

### ASSISTANT — D197-A02
No, the order is not cancelled immediately; it remains open while awaiting payment. However, if a payment slip must be reissued for this order, that new slip will be valid for only 3 business days before the order is cancelled automatically. Would you like me to issue a new payment slip now?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D197-A03
You are welcome. Your next step is to check your account or wait for an e-mail with a new payment slip that is due in 3 business days. Support answers between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further help.
