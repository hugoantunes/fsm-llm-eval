# D012

## Success criterion

The agent handles the return, states the 30-day window and refund timing, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Dialogue

### USER
I would like to cancel and also return the coat from NL-20261016, delivered last week. lina.hoffman@example.com

Thank you for contacting Northlight Store regarding order NL-20261016 for lina.hoffman@example.com. Since this order has already been delivered, we cannot cancel it but can process a return for the coat; could you please confirm if you would prefer an exchange or a refund?

### ASSISTANT — D012-A02
The refund will be issued within 10 business days after your returned coat reaches our warehouse. Please ensure the item is unused with tags attached and in its original packaging before sending it back.
