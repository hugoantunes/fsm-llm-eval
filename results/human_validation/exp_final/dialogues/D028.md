# D028

## Success criterion

The agent states that after dispatch the order can no longer be cancelled; the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

### USER
NL-20260728 has already been dispatched. I wanted it cancelled. tomas.berk@example.com

Since your order NL-20260728 for tomas.berk@example.com has already been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. You may instead refuse the delivery or open a return once it arrives. Would you like to proceed with a return or an exchange?

After dispatch, the order cannot be cancelled. Your options are to refuse the delivery when it arrives or to open a return within 30 days of receipt. Please also let me know the reason for your return, such as if the size is too small.
