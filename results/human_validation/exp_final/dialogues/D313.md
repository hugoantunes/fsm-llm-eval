# D313

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order number and e-mail from a vague opening, then states the dispatch and standard delivery estimates.

## Reference answer

An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Expected facts

- F09 — An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval.
- F10 — Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Dialogue

### USER
this delivery is taking forever

### ASSISTANT — D313-A01
I understand your concern about the delay. To help you, please provide the order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase.

### USER
NL-20260719 and a.hassan@example.com — that's everything I have

### ASSISTANT — D313-A02
Thank you for confirming; your order is NL-20260719 and was placed with a.hassan@example.com. Could you tell me which item of the order is delayed and what happened when it arrived or hasn't arrived yet?

### USER
when does it ship and how long is delivery

### ASSISTANT — D313-A03
Orders are dispatched within two business days of payment approval, followed by five to eight business days for standard delivery. If your package has not moved after seven business days since dispatch, please note that a delivery incident is registered and the carrier will investigate within five business days.

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D313-A04
Thank you for chatting with us today. Please remember that support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to return or exchange an item, and we look forward to assisting you further.
