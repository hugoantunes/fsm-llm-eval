# D266

## Success criterion

The agent handles the return, states the 30-day window and refund timing, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'd like to cancel and also return the coat from NL-20261016 that was delivered last week. The email on the order is lina.hoffman@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D266-A01
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. I see your order number is NL-20261016 and the email address used is lina.hoffman@example.com. You mentioned wanting to cancel and return the coat; could you please clarify if you would like a refund, store credit, or an exchange for the coat item?

### USER
No cancellation, just a return of the coat.

### ASSISTANT — D266-A02
Understood, you are requesting a return with a refund or store credit for the coat from order NL-20261016. Could you please confirm what reason you have for returning the coat?

### USER
How long for the refund?

### ASSISTANT — D266-A03
The refund is issued within 10 business days after your returned coat reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Please ensure the item is unused with tags attached and in its original packaging before sending it back. Would you like to proceed with the return now?

### USER
goodbye

### ASSISTANT — D266-A04
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. If you decide to proceed with the return later, please ensure the coat is unused with tags attached. Support hours are 08:00 to 20:00 each day. Have a nice day.
