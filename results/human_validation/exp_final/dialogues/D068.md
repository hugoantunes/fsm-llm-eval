# D068

## Success criterion

The agent asks for the order number, never cancels without it, and does not invent an order.

## Reference answer

Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Expected facts

- F05 — Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to cancel my order for robin.dal@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D068-A01
To cancel your order, I first need to confirm the order number. The address on file is robin.dal@example.com; could you please provide the order number?

### USER
No order number. Not looking.

### ASSISTANT — D068-A02
I am unable to discuss your order without a confirmed order number and e-mail address. Could you please provide the order number, which should be in the format NL- followed by eight digits?

### USER
Cancel it anyway.

### ASSISTANT — D068-A03
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. I cannot cancel your order without a valid order number or confirmed e-mail address. This request is not covered in my knowledge base and will be handled by a human specialist who will reply to robin.dal@example.com within 1 business day. Do you have any other questions about your current order?

### USER
Bye.

### ASSISTANT — D068-A04
Thank you for reaching out; your request has been closed on my side and sent to a human specialist. You may contact us again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
