# D318

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague "payment problem" opening and says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hey, the payment thing failed and I can't finish buying.

### ASSISTANT — D318-A01
I am sorry to hear your payment failed. Can you please provide the order number or the e-mail address used for the purchase so I can check the status? If you don't have an order number yet because the payment is still pending, please tell me which item you were trying to buy and why the payment slip expired.

### USER
NL-20261124, slip expired, nico.baum@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D318-A02
Thank you for providing those details. I confirm that your order number is NL-20261124 and the e-mail used was nico.baum@example.com. Since the payment slip has expired, it can be reissued in your account history or by asking support, provided the order has not been cancelled yet. Would you like me to proceed with checking if a reissue is possible for this order?

### USER
how do I get a new slip

### ASSISTANT — D318-A03
You can request a new payment slip directly from here. Please confirm how many times a slip has already been reissued for this order, as it can be reissued at most twice before the order is cancelled. Once I know that, I will tell you if a new one is available and what the due date will be.

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D318-A04
Thank you for reaching out earlier regarding your expired slip for order NL-20261124. Since we did not confirm how many times the payment slip had already been reissued, I cannot state whether a new one is available right now. Please reply when you are ready to check that detail so I can assist you further. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00.
