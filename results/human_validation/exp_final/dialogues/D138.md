# D138

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order number and e-mail from a vague opening, then states the dispatch and standard delivery estimates.

## Reference answer

An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Expected facts

- F09 — An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval.
- F10 — Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Dialogue

### USER
this delivery is taking forever

### ASSISTANT — D138-A01
Hello there. I can certainly look into your delivery status for you. Could you please tell me what specific request or help you need regarding this order?

### USER
NL-20260719 and a.hassan@example.com — that's everything I have

### ASSISTANT — D138-A02
Thank you, Mr. Hassan; the order number is NL-20260719 and the email address is a.hassan@example.com. To help you best, could you please confirm if your request is to track this order, start an exchange or return, cancel it, or reissue a payment slip?

### USER
when does it ship and how long is delivery

### ASSISTANT — D138-A03
Orders dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval typically take 5 to 8 business days for standard delivery. Would you like me to check the current status of your specific order?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D138-A04
Thank you, Mr. Hassan, and I will close this request on my end. You may return between 08:00 and 20:00 for further assistance.
