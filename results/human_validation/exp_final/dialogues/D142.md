# D142

## Success criterion

The agent asks for the e-mail and does not reissue a slip without confirming identity.

## Reference answer

Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Expected facts

- F05 — Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Dialogue

### USER
Can I get a new slip for NL-20260130?

### ASSISTANT — D142-A01
I can help you with a new payment slip, but first I need to confirm your order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase. You mentioned NL-20260130; could you please also provide the e-mail associated with that order?

### USER
I don't have the mail for that one.

### ASSISTANT — D142-A02
The order number must be followed by eight digits, so NL-20260130 is correct in format. Since we cannot discuss this request without the e-mail address used for the purchase, please check your confirmation email or account history to find it.

### USER
reissue anyway

### ASSISTANT — D142-A03
I cannot reissue a slip without confirming the e-mail address first, as this information is required to locate your order. Please provide the e-mail associated with the purchase so we can proceed.

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D142-A04
Thank you for contacting us, and I will close this request on my side. You may reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you are able to find your confirmation e-mail address.
