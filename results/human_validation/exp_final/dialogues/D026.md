# D026

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, 24 hours after payment approval.

## Reference answer

An order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, which happens 24 hours after payment approval.

## Expected facts

- F30 — An order with a personalised item cannot be cancelled once production has started, which happens 24 hours after payment approval.

## Dialogue

### USER
I ordered a personalised mug two days ago, NL-20260809, nia.okonkwo@example.com, not shipped. Can I still cancel, and when does production start after payment approval?

### ASSISTANT — D026-A01
I cannot cancel your order for the personalised mug because production has already started. Once production begins 24 hours after payment approval, an order with a personalised item can no longer be cancelled.

### USER
Thank you, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D026-A02
Thank you, and have a good day. Our support team is available from 08:00 to 20:00 for any future needs.
