# D167

## Success criterion

The agent says that a points programme is not covered and escalates; it invents no points rule.

## Reference answer

A points programme is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com. If I do that, will I lose my loyalty points?

### ASSISTANT — D167-A01
I can confirm your request is about order NL-20260321, but our records don't show whether cancelling an order affects loyalty points. Since that detail isn't in our policy, I'll hand this part to a human specialist and let them explain how it works for you. Would you like me to proceed with the cancellation now, or should we wait for the specialist's note first?

### USER
How many points lost?

### ASSISTANT — D167-A02
I cannot provide the number of points lost because our policy does not cover how cancellations affect loyalty points. I will have a human specialist explain that to you. Would you like me to proceed with cancelling order NL-20260321 now, or wait for their reply?
