# D161

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support, and a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support. A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hello, my slip for NL-20260625 expired yesterday. Could you please issue a new one for cara.ng@example.com and let me know when the new due date is?

### ASSISTANT — D161-A01
I can reissue your payment slip for order NL-20260625, but I need to confirm that this order was placed with the e-mail cara.ng@example.com before I proceed. Once confirmed, a new slip will be issued and remain valid for three business days.

### USER
yes, reissue

### ASSISTANT — D161-A02
Your payment slip for order NL-20260625 has been reissued to cara.ng@example.com. The new due date is three business days from today. Please note that a payment slip can be reissued at most twice per order; if this was your second reissue, the order will be cancelled once the new slip expires.

### USER
thanks, bye

### ASSISTANT — D161-A03
Thank you for contacting us. Your slip is now active and due in three business days. If you need further assistance, our support team answers between 08:00 and 20:00.
