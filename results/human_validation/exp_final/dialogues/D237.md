# D237

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D237-A01
You are requesting a reissue of your payment slip for order NL-20260706 associated with luis.ferreira@example.com. Our policy allows a payment slip to be reissued at most twice for the same order; since this is the third request, we cannot issue another one and will need to escalate this matter to a human specialist. Would you like me to proceed with that escalation now?

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D237-A02
After the second reissue expires, the order is automatically cancelled. Since this is your third request for order NL-20260706, we cannot issue another slip and will need to escalate this situation to a human specialist. Would you like me to proceed with that escalation now?

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D237-A03
Thank you for contacting us. Since the order has been cancelled due to reaching the maximum number of reissues, this request has been escalated to a human specialist who will contact you by e-mail within 1 business day. Please note that support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00.
