# D158

## Success criterion

The agent says that collecting an order at a store is not covered and escalates; it does not claim stores exist or do not exist beyond the online-only operation.

## Reference answer

Pick-up at a store is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER
I would like to collect NL-20260315 in a store instead of delivery for yuki.tanaka@example.com.

To help you switch to store pickup, could you please tell me which city branch you prefer and confirm that the item is still needed? Please note that we only support deliveries within the country, so your selected store must be located here.

Our knowledge base does not contain a list of store locations or their operating hours, so I cannot provide the nearest pick-up point directly. Please hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you further.

Nearest pick-up point, please.

### ASSISTANT — D158-A03
I cannot provide a list of store locations or identify the nearest pick-up point from our available data. Since this specific information is not in my knowledge base, I need to hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you with finding a store location.

Thank you for contacting Northlight Store support today. If you need assistance finding a store location for your pickup, please reach out to a human specialist when you return, as our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day.
