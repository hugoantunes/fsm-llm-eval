# D123

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Expected facts

- F29 — An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip for NL-20260920 is past its due date and I have not paid. hugo.stein@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D123-A01
Your order is NL-20260920, purchased with email hugo.stein@example.com. You are asking to reissue the payment slip for this expired order. Is that correct?

### USER
No, I am not asking to reissue a slip. Is the order cancelled automatically, and after how many days?

### ASSISTANT — D123-A02
An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date. The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Since your request is not covered by our standard guidelines regarding reissues or cancellations in this context, I will escalate it to a human specialist who will reply by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D123-A03
Thank you, and the request is closed on our side. The human specialist will reach out by e-mail within one business day. Our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00 each day.
