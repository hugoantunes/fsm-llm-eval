# D011

## Success criterion

The agent draws out which item and the order details, then states the 30-day return window.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to send something back.

### ASSISTANT — D011-A01
To help you with your return, could you please provide the order number and the email address used for the purchase?

### USER
I need to send something back. Order NL-20260909, grey trainers, delivered 12 days ago, kai.anders@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D011-A02
Thank you for providing those details. Your order number is confirmed as NL-20260909 and the email used was kai.anders@example.com. Regarding your return of the grey trainers, please note that returns are accepted within 30 calendar days of delivery; since your item was delivered 12 days ago, you are still within this window. Could you please confirm if you would like a full refund or an exchange for a different size or color?

### USER
reason required?

### ASSISTANT — D011-A03
No reason is required for a standard return. However, please note that the item must be unused with its tags attached and in its original packaging. Would you like to proceed with a refund or an exchange?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D011-A04
Thank you for reaching out today. If you decide to proceed with your return, please let us know your choice of refund or exchange, and we will guide you through the next steps. Our support hours are from 08:00 to 20:00 each day, so feel free to contact us anytime within those times if you need further assistance.
