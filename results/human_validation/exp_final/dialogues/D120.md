# D120

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I was told order NL-20261011 was cancelled after the slip expired, but I still want the slip anyway at daria.kowal@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D120-A01
I cannot reissue an expired payment slip if the order has already been cancelled, as per our policy. When a slip expires without being paid for, the order is automatically cancelled three business days later, and no further slips can be issued. You would need to place a completely new order to get a valid payment slip. Would you like me to help you start that new order?

### USER
What should I do instead?

### ASSISTANT — D120-A02
You should place a new order on our website using the same email address, daria.kowal@example.com. This will generate a fresh payment slip with a new due date of three business days from now. Would you like assistance finding your previous items to add them to the cart?

### USER
ok bye

### ASSISTANT — D120-A03
Thank you for reaching out today. To confirm, please place a new order on our website using daria.kowal@example.com to receive a fresh payment slip. Our support team is available daily from 08:00 to 20:00 if you have any further questions. Have a great day.
