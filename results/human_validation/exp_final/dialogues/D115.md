# D115

## Success criterion

The agent handles the return, states the 30-day window and refund timing, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I would like to cancel and also return the coat from order NL-20261016 which was delivered last week. My email is lina.hoffman@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D115-A01
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Before we proceed, I confirm your order number is NL-20261016 and the e-mail address is lina.hoffman@example.com. 

You mentioned wanting to cancel and also return the coat; since the order has already been delivered, a cancellation is no longer possible. Would you like to proceed with just returning the coat?

### USER
No, I'm not looking for a cancellation anymore, only a return of the coat.

### ASSISTANT — D115-A02
Since the order was delivered last week, please confirm if your reason for the return is that the size is too small or another issue. Also, let me know if you prefer an exchange, a refund, or store credit for this return.

### USER
How long for the refund?

### ASSISTANT — D115-A03
A refund is issued within 10 business days after your returned coat reaches our warehouse and passes inspection.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D115-A04
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; your request regarding the return is now closed on our side. You can expect a response within 10 business days once we receive the item. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need to reach us again before then.
