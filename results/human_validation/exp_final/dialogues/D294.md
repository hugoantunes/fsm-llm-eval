# D294

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support, and a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support. A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Slip for NL-20260625 expired. cara.ng@example.com. New slip, and when is it due?

I see your request regarding order NL-20260625 for the e-mail cara.ng@example.com. I can reissue an expired payment slip, but please confirm whether you would like me to proceed with that action now. A new slip will be due 3 business days after it is issued.

### USER
Yes, reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D294-A02
Your new payment slip for order NL-20260625 has been issued and you can find it in your account history or by asking support again. It is due within 3 business days from now. Thank you for confirming, and our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00.
