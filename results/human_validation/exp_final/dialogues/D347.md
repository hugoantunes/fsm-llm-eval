# D347

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Expected facts

- F36 — An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Dialogue

### USER
I have order NL-20260903 awaiting payment slip, matt.owens@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D347-A01
You are requesting the reissue of a payment slip for order NL-20260903 associated with matt.owens@example.com; is that correct?

### USER
How long does unpaid stock stay reserved, and what happens to the items after that?

### ASSISTANT — D347-A02
An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue. If your slip remains unpaid past this time, the order will be cancelled automatically unless a new one is placed.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D347-A03
Thank you, and I am closing this request on my side. You may contact us again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
