# D175

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague opening, confirms identity, and states free cancellation before dispatch plus the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled before dispatch with no fee, refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
something's wrong, I want to undo an order

### ASSISTANT — D175-A01
I can help you with that, but first please confirm the order number and the email address used for the purchase. Also, has your order been dispatched yet?

### USER
ina.berg@example.com and then NL-20261118, nothing shipped

### ASSISTANT — D175-A02
Thank you, I have noted your order NL-20261118 for ina.berg@example.com which has not yet been dispatched. Since the order is still preparing, it can be cancelled without a fee and the refund will appear within 5 business days. Would you like to proceed with cancelling the entire order?

### USER
how long for the money

### ASSISTANT — D175-A03
The refund will be issued back to your original payment method within 5 business days once the cancellation is confirmed. Do you want me to finalize the cancellation now?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D175-A04
Thank you, and goodbye. If you need to return in the future, our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00.
