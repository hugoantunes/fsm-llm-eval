# D238

## Success criterion

The agent handles the reissue request, then states the 3-business-day stock hold when the subject changes.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Expected facts

- F36 — An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need a new slip. Can you reissue it? The previous one, NL-20260207, has expired.

### ASSISTANT — D238-A01
I can help with that, but I need to confirm which e-mail address was used for the purchase of order NL-20260207 before I proceed.

### USER
olga.petrova@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D238-A02
Thank you, Olga. I have confirmed your order number is NL-20260207 and the e-mail address is olga.petrova@example.com. Since this slip has expired, I will reissue it for you now; please note that the new payment slip is due in 3 business days.

### USER
before you finish — how long do the items stay reserved?

### ASSISTANT — D238-A03
Items are held in stock for 3 business days while waiting for payment, after which they return to the catalogue. Is there anything else I can help you with regarding your order?
