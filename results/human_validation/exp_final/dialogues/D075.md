# D075

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D075-A01
I cannot reissue a third payment slip for order NL-20260706 because our policy allows at most two reissues per order; after the second one expires, the order is cancelled. Please place a new order instead.

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D075-A02
After the second payment slip expires and remains unpaid, the order is automatically cancelled and cannot be recovered. You would need to place a completely new order for these items.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D075-A03
Thank you, Luis. Goodbye. Our support hours are 08:00 to 20:00 in case you need further assistance later.
