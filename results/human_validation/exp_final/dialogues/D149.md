# D149

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D149-A01
You are requesting a reissue of the payment slip for order NL-20260706 associated with luis.ferreira@example.com. Our policy allows for a maximum of two reissues per order; since this is your third request, I cannot issue it and will need to hand this over to a human specialist.

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D149-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. After the second reissue expires, the order is cancelled; you would need to place a new order instead. I will escalate this request to a human specialist who can assist further.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D149-A03
Thank you for contacting us. Your request has been closed and will be handled by a human specialist. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to come back.
