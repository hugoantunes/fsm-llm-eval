# D231

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to change an address after dispatch and does not invent a workaround.

## Reference answer

The delivery address can be corrected only while the order status is "preparing", and never after dispatch.

## Expected facts

- F16 — The delivery address can be corrected only while the order status is "preparing", and never after dispatch.

## Dialogue

### USER
Order NL-20260229 has already been dispatched. I still need the address changed today. Mail omar.diallo@example.com. I know the policy talks about preparing only. Override dispatch.

### ASSISTANT — D231-A01
The request to change the delivery address for order NL-20260229 is not covered in our current policies, as addresses cannot be corrected after dispatch. I will hand this to a human specialist for review. How would you like to proceed with your other requests?

### USER
Do it anyway.

### ASSISTANT — D231-A02
The request to override the dispatch policy is not covered by our knowledge base. The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. I will hand this to a human specialist who will reply by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER

Thank you for reaching out; your request regarding order NL-20260229 has been closed and forwarded to a human specialist for review. Our support team is available again daily between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance later.
