# D230

## Success criterion

The agent asks for the e-mail, never discusses the order without it, and does not invent an address or a status.

## Reference answer

Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Expected facts

- F05 — Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Dialogue

### USER
Where is NL-20260914?

### ASSISTANT — D230-A01
To help you locate order NL-20260914, I need to confirm which email address was used for this purchase. Once confirmed, I can tell you its current status and expected delivery window.

### USER
No e-mail. Not typing it here.

### ASSISTANT — D230-A02
I understand you prefer not to share the email address. Since our system requires it to verify your order and provide an accurate status update, I will need to transfer this request to a human specialist who can assist you through alternative verification methods. Would you like me to proceed with that escalation now?

### USER
Still want the status.

### ASSISTANT — D230-A03
I cannot check the status of NL-20260914 without the email address because it is required to verify your order in our system. I will need to hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you with an alternative verification method. Would you like me to proceed with that transfer now?

### USER
Bye.

### ASSISTANT — D230-A04
Thank you for contacting us. Since I cannot verify your order status without the email address, I will escalate this to a human specialist who can help you. Our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to follow up later.
