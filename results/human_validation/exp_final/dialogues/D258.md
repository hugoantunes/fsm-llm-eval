# D258

## Success criterion

The agent states that a returned item must be unused, with tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Reference answer

A returned item must be unused, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Expected facts

- F19 — A returned item must be unused, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Dialogue

### USER
can I send a jacket back?

### ASSISTANT — D258-A01
I can look into your return, but please provide the order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase.

### USER
I already wore it once around the house.

### ASSISTANT — D258-A02
Underwear, swimwear and earrings cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken; however, jackets generally can be returned if they are unused with tags attached and in original packaging. Since you have worn this jacket, it may not meet our return conditions. Could you please provide the order number and the e-mail address associated with the purchase so we can check your specific order details?

### USER
order NL-20260127, peter.novak@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D258-A03
Thank you, Peter. I confirm this request is for a return regarding order NL-20260127 purchased with the e-mail peter.novak@example.com. Since you mentioned wearing the jacket, please note that items must be unused with tags attached and in original packaging to be eligible for return; could you confirm if your other items meet these conditions?

### USER
do I need the tags and the original box?

### ASSISTANT — D258-A04
Yes, all returned items must be unused, with their tags attached and in their original packaging. Since the jacket has been worn, it does not meet these conditions. Would you like to proceed with an exchange or store credit instead, or would you prefer to speak with a specialist about your options?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D258-A05
Thank you, Peter. Your request regarding the return is now closed on our side due to the item having been worn. If you need further assistance in the future, please contact us between 08:00 and 20:00.
