# D016

## Success criterion

The agent states that a cancellation refund goes to the original payment method, does not agree to convert it into store credit, and escalates that extra request.

## Reference answer

A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days. Converting that refund into store credit is not covered and is escalated.

## Expected facts

- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to cancel order NL-20260506 before dispatch and put the money on account credit, not the card.

### ASSISTANT — D016-A01
Before we proceed with your cancellation request, could you please provide the e-mail address used for this purchase? Please note that cancellations are refunded only to the original payment method.

### USER
Irene.pohl@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D016-A02
You want to cancel order NL-20260506 purchased with Irene.pohl@example.com and receive the refund as store credit. Is that correct? Please note that cancellations are refunded only to the original payment method.

### USER
no, I need store credit instead of the card refund.

### ASSISTANT — D016-A03
I understand you would like store credit, but our policy is that cancellations are refunded only to the original payment method used for the purchase. A change to store credit for a cancellation is not covered in my knowledge base and will need to be handled by a human specialist. Would you like me to escalate this request?

### USER
yes, please escalate to a human specialist.

### ASSISTANT — D016-A04
I have escalated your request regarding the cancellation of order NL-20260506 for Irene.pohl@example.com and the alternative refund method to a human specialist. They will contact you by e-mail within one business day.
