# D235

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to cancel after dispatch and points to refusing delivery or opening a return; it does not override dispatch.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

- F27 — After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Dialogue

### USER
I want to cancel NL-20260412 even though dispatched for zoe.martin@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D235-A01
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. I have read back order number NL-20260412 and the e-mail address zoe.martin@example.com. Orders can no longer be cancelled once they are dispatched; can you confirm if your goal is to refuse the delivery or open a return instead?

### USER
I will not refuse delivery and I will not open a return. Cancel it.

### ASSISTANT — D235-A02
After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled; the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return. Since you have declined both options, this request is not covered by our standard policies and requires human specialist assistance.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D235-A03
Thank you for speaking with us, and I will close this request on my side now. The support team answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need to return in the future.
