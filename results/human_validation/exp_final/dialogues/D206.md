# D206

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support, and a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support. A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
My slip for NL-20260625 expired yesterday, cara.ng@example.com. Could you please issue a new one and let me know when it is due?

### ASSISTANT — D206-A01
I have noted your order number NL-20260625 and the email address cara.ng@example.com. I can reissue an expired payment slip for you, but please confirm which order item you are trying to purchase so I can verify the request details.

### USER
Yes, reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D206-A02
I have reissued your payment slip for order NL-20260625. The new slip is due in 3 business days from today. You will see it listed in your account history shortly.

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D206-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. Your new payment slip for order NL-20260625 is due in 3 business days. Our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00.
