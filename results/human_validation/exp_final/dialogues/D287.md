# D287

## Success criterion

The agent says one item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately, offers to cancel the whole unshipped order at no fee, and states the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

A single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled on its own. Before dispatch the whole order can be cancelled at no fee, and the refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F31 — Cancelling an order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.
- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to undo part of my order but I am not sure which one.

### ASSISTANT — D287-A01
To discuss your order, please confirm the order number and the e-mail address used for purchase. Regarding your request, I cannot cancel just one item from a multi-item order; all items must be cancelled together before dispatch or handled via a return after delivery.

### USER
my email is customer@example.com but I have no number yet

### ASSISTANT — D287-A02
To locate your order, please check the purchase confirmation e-mail sent to customer@example.com for the order number in its format NL- followed by eight digits. If you cannot find it, a human specialist will need that number to assist you further.

### USER
I found it: NL-20260145. It has two items and nothing shipped so I want to drop only one.

### ASSISTANT — D287-A03
For order NL-20260145 purchased with customer@example.com, please confirm if you want to cancel the entire order or return just the single item after delivery.

### USER
I need to drop only one, how long for the money? bye

### ASSISTANT — D287-A04
A full refund is issued within 5 business days if we cancel the entire order before dispatch. Returning a single item will take 10 business days after it reaches the warehouse and passes inspection. I cannot process dropping just one item yet; would you like to proceed with cancelling the whole order or keep it?
