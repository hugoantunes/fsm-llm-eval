# D053

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague opening, confirms identity, and states free cancellation before dispatch plus the 5-day refund.

## Reference answer

An order can be cancelled before dispatch with no fee, refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F26 — An order can be cancelled by the customer at any time before dispatch, with no fee.
- F28 — A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
something's wrong, I want to undo an order

### ASSISTANT — D053-A01
To help you with your request, I need to confirm your order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase.

### USER
it was ina.berg@example.com and then NL-20261118, nothing shipped

### ASSISTANT — D053-A02
You want to cancel order NL-20261118 purchased with ina.berg@example.com; is that correct?

### USER
how long for the money

### ASSISTANT — D053-A03
A cancellation before dispatch is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days. Did you also need to provide a reason for the return or exchange, since that is required if this was an exchange request?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D053-A04
Thank you for your message; your order has been closed on our side. If you have any further questions, please contact us between 08:00 and 20:00.
