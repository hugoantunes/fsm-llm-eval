# D114

## Success criterion

The agent treats tracking as the request, confirms identity, says where the tracking code is, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Expected facts

- F12 — The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Dialogue

### USER
tracking code for NL-20260803 please, and I might cancel if it's late. priya.shah@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D114-A01
You are requesting a tracking code for order NL-20260803; is your primary goal to track this shipment or to cancel the order? The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country.

### USER
Just tracking for now, not a cancel.

### ASSISTANT — D114-A02
The tracking code has been sent to priya.shah@example.com at the time of dispatch. Your order was dispatched within two business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes five to eight business days after that. You will see the tracking number in your account order history as well.

### USER
Where does the tracking code show up?

### ASSISTANT — D114-A03
The tracking code appears in the order history section of your customer account and was also sent by e-mail at the time of dispatch. If you need further assistance with your account, please let me know.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D114-A04
Thank you for reaching out to Northlight Store; your request regarding order NL-20260803 is now closed on our side. You may return anytime between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
