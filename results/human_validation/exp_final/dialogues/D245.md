# D245

## Success criterion

The agent states that a returned item must be unused, with tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Reference answer

A returned item must be unused, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Expected facts

- F19 — A returned item must be unused, with its tags attached and in its original packaging.

## Dialogue

### USER
can I send a jacket back?

### ASSISTANT — D245-A01
Yes, you can return a jacket if it is unused, has its tags attached, and comes in the original packaging within 30 days of delivery. To proceed with your return, please confirm the order number and the email address used for the purchase.

### USER
I already wore it once around the house

### ASSISTANT — D245-A02
Since the jacket was worn, it does not meet our condition that returned items must be unused and in their original packaging. Therefore, we cannot process this return. I can hand your request to a human specialist who may be able to assist you further.

### USER
order NL-20260127, peter.novak@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D245-A03
Thank you for confirming order NL-20260127 and the email address. Since the jacket has been worn, it does not meet our requirement for returns to be unused with tags attached. I will now escalate this request to a human specialist who can review your specific situation.

### USER
do I need the tags and the original box?

### ASSISTANT — D245-A04
Yes, any item returned must be unused with its tags attached and in its original packaging. If these conditions are not met, we cannot process the return ourselves. Would you like me to hand your request to a human specialist who can review this exception for you?
