# D032

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order number and e-mail from a vague opening, then states the dispatch and standard delivery estimates.

## Reference answer

An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Expected facts

- F09 — An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval.
- F10 — Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.

## Dialogue

### USER
this delivery is taking forever

### ASSISTANT — D032-A01
I'm sorry to hear your delivery is delayed. To help you, could you please share the order number and the e-mail address used for the purchase?

### USER
NL-20260719 and a.hassan@example.com — that's everything I have

### ASSISTANT — D032-A02
Thank you, Mr. Hassan; your order is NL-20260719 placed with a.hassan@example.com. Was this order sent via standard or express delivery?

### USER
when does it ship and how long is delivery

### ASSISTANT — D032-A03
Orders ship within 2 business days of payment approval, followed by 5 to 8 business days for standard delivery. Did you select express delivery, which takes only 2 business days but costs an additional fee?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D032-A04
Thank you for your message. You can return anytime between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance regarding your order.
