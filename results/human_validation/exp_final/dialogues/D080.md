# D080

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D080-A01
I cannot issue a third payment slip because our policy allows reissuing at most twice for the same order. Since this would be the third attempt, your order will now be cancelled automatically. Would you like to place a new order instead?

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D080-A02
After the second reissue expires, the order is cancelled. Since your payment slip has now expired for the third time, your order NL-20260706 will be cancelled and you can place a new order if you wish to proceed.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D080-A03
Thank you for contacting us. If you decide to place a new order later, please feel free to reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day.
