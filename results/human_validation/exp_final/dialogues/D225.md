# D225

## Success criterion

The agent answers the damaged-item policy after the subject change and does not drop the identity check.

## Reference answer

An item that arrives damaged or incorrect is collected at no cost, and the customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund.

## Expected facts

- F25 — An item that arrives damaged or incorrect is collected at no cost, and the customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'd like to exchange my green shirt for a larger size. The order number is NL-20261202 and my email is amira.saleh@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D225-A01
You want to exchange item from order NL-20261202 sent to amira.saleh@example.com; please confirm this request. Does your order include a green shirt?

### USER
Actually it arrived with a tear. Damaged-item path, not a size swap.

### ASSISTANT — D225-A02
That is an item arriving damaged or incorrect, which we can handle at no cost. Would you prefer a replacement or a full refund for this order?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D225-A03
Thank you for your patience; your request regarding the damaged item is now closed on our side. If you need further assistance, our support team is available to answer messages between 08:00 and 20:00.
