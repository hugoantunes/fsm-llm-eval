# D218

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Reference answer

A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Expected facts

- F34 — A payment slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order; after the second reissue expires the order is cancelled.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip expired again. This is the third one I need for NL-20260706. luis.ferreira@example.com. Can you issue it?

### ASSISTANT — D218-A01
I cannot issue a third reissued payment slip because our policy allows at most two reissues per order; after the second, the order is cancelled. To proceed, please confirm if you would like to place a new order with us.

### USER
What happens after the second reissue expires?

### ASSISTANT — D218-A02
After the second reissue expires, the order is cancelled and no further payment slips can be issued for it. If you wish to purchase these items, please place a new order instead.

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D218-A03
Thank you for contacting us today. Since your order NL-20260706 has been cancelled due to the expired payment slip limit, you will need to place a new order if you wish to purchase the items. Our support hours are 08:00 to 20:00.
