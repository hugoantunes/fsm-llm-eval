# D220

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Expected facts

- F36 — An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Dialogue

### USER
I have order NL-20260903 awaiting payment slip, matt.owens@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D220-A01
Thank you, Matt. I see your order is currently waiting for a payment slip. Would you like me to reissue the payment slip for this order?

### USER
How long does unpaid stock stay reserved, and what happens to the items after that?

### ASSISTANT — D220-A02
Unpaid stock stays reserved for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue. Would you like me to reissue the payment slip for your order now?

### USER
Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D220-A03
You are welcome, Matt. Have a good day. Our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need us again.
