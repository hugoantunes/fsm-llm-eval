# D129

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Expected facts

- F29 — An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip for NL-20260920 is past its due date and I have not paid. hugo.stein@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D129-A01
You are asking to reissue the expired payment slip for order NL-20260920 purchased with hugo.stein@example.com. Is that correct?

### USER
Is the order cancelled automatically, and after how many days?

### ASSISTANT — D129-A02
The order awaiting a payment slip is held for 3 business days, after which it is cancelled if not paid. If you have not paid within that time, the system cancels the order automatically. Would you like me to reissue the payment slip now?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D129-A03
Thank you, and this request is now closed on our side. Please note that if an order has been cancelled due to a missed payment deadline, a new order must be placed; we do not have the ability to reactivate a cancelled one. Our support team answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need further assistance.
