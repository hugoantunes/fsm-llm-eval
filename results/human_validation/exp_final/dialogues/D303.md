# D303

## Success criterion

The agent says that collecting an order at a store is not covered and escalates; it does not claim stores exist or do not exist beyond the online-only operation.

## Reference answer

Pick-up at a store is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
I would like to collect NL-20260315 in a store instead of delivery for yuki.tanaka@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D303-A01
You are asking to collect your order NL-20260315 in-store rather than have it delivered, is that correct? Please note that this request falls outside the four types of assistance we can handle directly, so I will need to connect you with a human specialist.

### USER
Nearest pick-up point?

### ASSISTANT — D303-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. A human specialist will get back to you by e-mail regarding your collection request.

### USER
Nearest pick-up point, please.

### ASSISTANT — D303-A03
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. A human specialist will address your question about a pick-up location and get back to you by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D303-A04
Thank you for reaching out today; we have closed this request on our end. You can reach us again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
