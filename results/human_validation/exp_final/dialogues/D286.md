# D286

## Success criterion

The agent confirms the order number and the e-mail, states the dispatch and standard delivery estimates, says where the tracking code is, and closes.

## Reference answer

An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval, and standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch. The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Expected facts

- F09 — An order is dispatched within 2 business days of payment approval.
- F10 — Standard delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after dispatch.
- F12 — The tracking code is sent by e-mail at dispatch and also appears in the order history of the customer's account.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi — order NL-20260211, e-mail jordan.lee@example.com. When does it arrive and where is the tracking code?

### ASSISTANT — D286-A01
Your order was dispatched within two business days of payment approval, so standard delivery will take five to eight business days after dispatch. The tracking code was sent by e-mail at that time and also appears in your account order history.

### USER
Yes, that's the one. Dispatch time and the tracking code, please.

### ASSISTANT — D286-A02
The order status is currently updating; I cannot confirm the exact dispatch date or retrieve the tracking code from my records right now. Please open a ticket with our specialist team who will check these details for you.

### USER
Thanks. That's all.

### ASSISTANT — D286-A03
Thank you, and please come back between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
