# D217

## Success criterion

The agent says the store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, does not take a restaurant booking, and offers to escalate.

## Reference answer

Northlight Store sells clothing, footwear and home goods online. A restaurant booking is not covered and is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F01 — Northlight Store sells clothing, footwear and home goods online, and delivers only inside the country.
- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
book a restaurant tonight

### ASSISTANT — D217-A01
I am unable to book restaurants or make reservations outside of Northlight Store's services. Please let me know if you need help with your clothing, footwear, or home goods orders.

Northlight Store does not offer memberships or free shipping programs; shipping costs apply to all orders unless a specific promotion is active. If you need details about current promotions or delivery options, I can provide those based on our policies. How else can I assist you today?

### USER
No order. Restaurant.

### ASSISTANT — D217-A03
I cannot book restaurants or handle reservations outside of Northlight Store services. Please let me know if you need assistance with an existing clothing, footwear, or home goods order instead.

### USER

Thank you for contacting us today. If you need to return or exchange an item from a Northlight Store order, please reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day.
