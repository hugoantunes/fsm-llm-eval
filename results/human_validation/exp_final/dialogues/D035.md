# D035

## Success criterion

The agent asks for the e-mail and does not reissue a slip without confirming identity.

## Reference answer

Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Expected facts

- F05 — Before discussing an order, the agent confirms the order number and the e-mail address used in the purchase.

## Dialogue

### USER
Can I get a new slip for NL-20260130?

### ASSISTANT — D035-A01
To help you with your request, could you please confirm the e-mail address used for order NL-20260130?

### USER
No mail.

### ASSISTANT — D035-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. A reissued payment slip can be issued within our standard procedures if you have a valid order; however, since no e-mail address was provided or confirmed, I cannot proceed with this request. Please confirm your purchase e-mail so a human specialist may assist you further.

### USER
Please reissue anyway.

### ASSISTANT — D035-A03
I cannot reissue the payment slip without first confirming the e-mail address used for the purchase. As this is not covered by my current instructions, I will hand this request to a human specialist who can assist you.

### USER
Bye.

### ASSISTANT — D035-A04
Thank you for your message. Your request regarding order NL-20260130 has been closed and forwarded to a human specialist who will contact you by e-mail within 1 business day if possible. You may reach us again between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need further assistance.
