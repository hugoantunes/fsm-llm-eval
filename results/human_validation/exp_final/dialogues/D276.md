# D276

## Success criterion

The agent says that data retention after account deletion is not covered, invents no retention period, and escalates.

## Reference answer

Data retention is not covered and is escalated to a human specialist. Invent no retention period.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, could you please reissue slip NL-20260722 for paul.nguyen@example.com?

### ASSISTANT — D276-A01
You are asking to have the payment slip for order NL-20260722 reissued to paul.nguyen@example.com. Is that correct?

### USER
Yes, and how long do you keep my personal data after I delete my account?

### ASSISTANT — D276-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. The request about data retention after account deletion is not covered here, so a human specialist will answer you by e-mail within 1 business day. Would you like to proceed with reissuing the slip instead?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D276-A03
Thank you, and this request is closed on our side. The payment slip will be reissued as discussed, and support hours are from 08:00 to 20:00 for any future questions.
