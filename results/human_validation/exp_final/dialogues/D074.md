# D074

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague "payment problem" opening and says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

### USER

I understand you're having trouble completing your purchase because of a payment issue. To help you, I'll need your order number and the email address used for the purchase if this is for an existing order. If you haven't placed the order yet, please let me know which items you'd like to buy so I can assist with the next steps.

You can get a new slip by checking your order history in your account or by asking support directly. If you ask support, I will reissue it for you right away. Would you like me to proceed with the reissue now?
