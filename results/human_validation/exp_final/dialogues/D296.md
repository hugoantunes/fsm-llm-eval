# D296

## Success criterion

The agent states that a slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need help with order NL-20261011. It was cancelled after the slip expired, but I still want the slip anyway. daria.kowal@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D296-A01
You are requesting the reissue of an expired payment slip for order NL-20261011, which was placed with the e-mail address daria.kowal@example.com. The store policy states that a payment slip can be reissued at most twice; since your original order was cancelled after the slip expired, we cannot reissue a slip for this specific order and you would need to place a new one instead.

### USER
What should I do instead?

### ASSISTANT — D296-A02
You should place a new order instead. The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. I can help you start that new order if you would like.

### USER
ok bye

### ASSISTANT — D296-A03
Thank you for your message; your request regarding the original order is closed on our side. If you need further assistance, our support team answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00.
