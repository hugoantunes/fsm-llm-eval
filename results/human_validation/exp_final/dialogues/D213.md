# D213

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support, and a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support. A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Slip for NL-20260625 expired. cara.ng@example.com. New slip, and when is it due?

### ASSISTANT — D213-A01
You are requesting a reissue of the payment slip for order NL-20260625 purchased with cara.ng@example.com. The store states that a reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued. Would you like to proceed with this request?

### USER
Yes, reissue.

### ASSISTANT — D213-A02
The order number NL-20260625 and the e-mail address cara.ng@example.com are confirmed for this payment slip reissue. I will process the request now, but please note that a new slip can be reissued at most twice for the same order. The reissued slip will be due 3 business days after issuance.

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D213-A03
Thank you for your message; this request is closed on our side. You may contact us again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
