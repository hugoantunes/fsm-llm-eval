# D086

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to cancel after dispatch and points to refusing delivery or opening a return; it does not override dispatch.

## Reference answer

After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Expected facts

- F27 — After dispatch an order can no longer be cancelled: the customer refuses the delivery or opens a return.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need to cancel order NL-20260412 even though it dispatched for zoe.martin@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D086-A01
You are asking to cancel order NL-20260412 sent to zoe.martin@example.com, but our policy states that orders cannot be cancelled after dispatch. Is this the only reason you need support today?

### USER
I will not refuse delivery and I will not open a return. Cancel it.

### ASSISTANT — D086-A02
Our policy does not allow us to cancel an order once it has been dispatched. The refund process begins automatically only after you refuse the delivery or open a return, which takes 10 business days. Would you like to proceed with opening a return instead?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D086-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; this request is now closed on our side. If you need further assistance in the future, please reach out between 08:00 and 20:00.
