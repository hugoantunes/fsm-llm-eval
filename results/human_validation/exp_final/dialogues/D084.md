# D084

## Success criterion

The agent handles the reissue request, then states the 3-business-day stock hold when the subject changes.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Expected facts

- F36 — An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need a new slip for my order, specifically NL-20260207 sent to olga.petrova@example.com since the previous one expired. Before you finish — how long do the items stay reserved?

### ASSISTANT — D084-A01
You are requesting a reissue of your payment slip for order NL-20260207 sent to olga.petrova@example.com. Before I proceed, could you please confirm that this is your correct request and not an exchange or return?

### USER
Yes, that's correct. I need a new slip for NL-20260207 because the previous one expired.

### ASSISTANT — D084-A02
Your payment slip has been reissued successfully. The new slip is due 3 business days after it was issued. Your order remains reserved until this deadline passes, and the stock will return to the catalogue if not paid by then.

### USER
Thank you! And just to confirm, how long do the items stay reserved?

### ASSISTANT — D084-A03
The items stay reserved for 3 business days after the payment slip is issued. If payment is not received by then, the stock returns to the catalogue.
