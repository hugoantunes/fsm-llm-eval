# D320

## Success criterion

The agent says an exchange for another size depends on stock, and when unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit valid for 12 months.

## Reference answer

An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit. Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F23 — An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit.
- F24 — Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Please exchange the blue jacket from NL-20260630 for a larger size. hana.weiss@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D320-A01
Thank you for your request to exchange the blue jacket on order NL-20260630. Before I proceed, please confirm that this was purchased with the email address hana.weiss@example.com. Also, could you tell me the reason for the exchange and which size you would like to receive instead?

### USER
If that size is not in stock, can I choose a refund or store credit, and how long does credit last?

Yes, if your chosen size is unavailable, you can choose between a refund or store credit. Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued. Would you like to proceed with an exchange for a different available size, or would you prefer one of those other options?
