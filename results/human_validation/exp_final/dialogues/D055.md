# D055

## Success criterion

The agent says that a points programme is not covered and escalates; it invents no points rule.

## Reference answer

A points programme is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I need to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com. If I do that, will I lose my loyalty points?

### ASSISTANT — D055-A01
You want to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com; please confirm that this is correct. Regarding your question about loyalty points, our knowledge base does not contain information on whether they are lost upon cancellation.

### USER
How many points lost?

### ASSISTANT — D055-A02
Our knowledge base does not specify how many loyalty points are lost when an order is cancelled. We can proceed with the cancellation, but we cannot provide details on point deductions. Would you like to continue with cancelling the order?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D055-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; your request regarding the cancellation has been closed on our side. If you need further assistance with points or other matters, please reach out between 08:00 and 20:00.
