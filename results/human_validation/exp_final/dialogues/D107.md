# D107

## Success criterion

The agent says an exchange for another size depends on stock, and when unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit valid for 12 months.

## Reference answer

An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit. Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F23 — An exchange for another size depends on stock, and when the size is unavailable the customer chooses between a refund and store credit.
- F24 — Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued.

### USER
Hello, I would like to exchange the blue jacket from NL-20260630 for a larger size. hana.weiss@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D107-A01
Thank you for your message. Before we proceed with the exchange request for the blue jacket on order NL-20260630, could you please confirm that this is the email address used for the purchase: hana.weiss@example.com?

If the larger size is unavailable, you can choose between a full refund or store credit as per our policy. Store credit is valid for 12 months from the day it is issued. Would you like to proceed with an exchange for the available size, or would you prefer the refund or store credit option?

You are welcome. If you decide to proceed with the exchange, please let me know your preference so I can guide you through the next steps. Our support team is available daily between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance. Thank you for choosing Northlight Store.
