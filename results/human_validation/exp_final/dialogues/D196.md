# D196

## Success criterion

The agent reissues the slip and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support. A reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Slip NL-20261213 expired; reissue it or I cancel. freya.lund@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D196-A01
You have requested to reissue the payment slip for order NL-20261213, which is linked to the email address freya.lund@example.com. The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. Would you like me to proceed with reissuing the slip?

### USER
Yes, please reissue it, not cancel.

### ASSISTANT — D196-A02
The expired payment slip for order NL-20261213 is being reissued in your account history now. This new slip will be due for payment within 3 business days of issuance. Please note that a payment slip can only be reissued twice for the same order before it is cancelled automatically.

### USER
When is the new slip due?

### ASSISTANT — D196-A03
The new payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued. You should check your account history or contact support if you need the exact date. I can also help with anything else regarding this order.

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D196-A04
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store; your request to reissue the slip is closed on our side. Support answers between 08:00 and 20:00, so please reach out within those hours if you need further assistance.
