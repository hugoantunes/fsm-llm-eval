# D112

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, treats a tracking code with no movement for 7 business days as a delivery incident investigated within 5 business days, and closes.

## Reference answer

A tracking code with no movement for 7 business days is registered as a delivery incident, which the carrier investigates within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F14 — A tracking code with no movement for 7 business days is registered as a delivery incident, which the carrier investigates within 5 business days.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hello, I have been watching the tracking page for order NL-20260418 for a full week. The code has not moved in 7 business days. The e-mail on the order is sam.okoye@example.com. I am not angry, I just need to know whether this is already a delivery incident and how long an investigation takes once you open one.

### ASSISTANT — D112-A01
Thank you for your patience with order NL-20260418 for sam.okoye@example.com; since the tracking code has not moved for 7 business days, this is indeed registered as a delivery incident. The carrier will investigate this matter within the following 5 business days.

### USER
Yes, seven business days with no movement. What happens next?

### ASSISTANT — D112-A02
After the carrier completes its investigation, they will confirm whether the parcel was successfully delivered or if it needs to be returned to the warehouse. If the parcel is returned after two failed delivery attempts, you will receive a full refund within 10 business days.

### USER
Thank you, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D112-A03
Thank you for contacting us; please remember that refunds are processed within 10 business days if your parcel is returned to the warehouse. Our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00 should you need to follow up on this later today.
