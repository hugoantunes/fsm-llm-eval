# D127

## Success criterion

The agent says that a points programme is not covered and escalates; it invents no points rule.

## Reference answer

A points programme is not covered. The request is escalated to a human specialist.

## Expected facts

- F03 — The agent states only what this knowledge base contains, and says plainly when a question is not covered here.
- F04 — A request this knowledge base does not cover is escalated to a human specialist, who replies by e-mail within 1 business day.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'd like to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com. If I do that, will I lose any loyalty points?

### ASSISTANT — D127-A01
I understand you want to cancel order NL-20260321 for ben.carter@example.com. Please confirm that cancellation is the only request you need right now.

Our records show this order was placed recently; please note that cancelling an order cancels all of its items and refunds them to your original payment method within 5 business days, but loyalty points are not covered by our current policies.

### USER
How many points lost?

### ASSISTANT — D127-A02
The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. This request regarding the specific number of lost points is not covered by our knowledge base, so I will escalate it to a human specialist who will reply by e-mail within 1 business day.

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D127-A03
Thank you for reaching out; your request is closed on my side and will be handled by a specialist regarding the points. Please note that our support hours are between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to contact us again.
