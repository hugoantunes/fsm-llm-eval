# D280

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity, treats a tracking code with no movement for 7 business days as a delivery incident investigated within 5 business days, and closes.

## Reference answer

A tracking code with no movement for 7 business days is registered as a delivery incident, which the carrier investigates within 5 business days.

## Expected facts

- F14 — A tracking code with no movement for 7 business days is registered as a delivery incident, which the carrier investigates within 5 business days.

### USER

I am not angry, I just need to know whether this is already a delivery incident and how long an investigation takes once you open one.

Your tracking code has been stationary for seven business days, which means it is now registered as a delivery incident. The carrier will investigate this matter within five business days. May I confirm that the email address on file for order NL-20260418 is sam.okoye@example.com?

Yes, seven business days with no movement. What happens next?

### ASSISTANT — D280-A02
The carrier will investigate this delivery incident within five business days. Would you like me to explain the possible outcomes once the investigation is complete?

You are welcome for your patience regarding order NL-20260418; our team will update you once the carrier's investigation concludes within five business days. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need to follow up later.
