# D285

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Expected facts

- F29 — An order awaiting a payment slip is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the slip's due date.

## Dialogue

### USER
The payment slip for NL-20260920 is past its due date and I have not paid. hugo.stein@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D285-A01
Your order number is NL-20260920 and the purchase was made with hugo.stein@example.com. Since the payment slip has expired, I can reissue a new slip for you; would you like me to do that?

### USER
Is the order cancelled automatically, and after how many days?

### ASSISTANT — D285-A02
Yes, the order is cancelled automatically 3 business days after the payment slip's due date if it remains unpaid. Would you like me to reissue a new payment slip for your order now?

### USER
Thanks, bye.

### ASSISTANT — D285-A03
You are welcome, and thank you for reaching out. If you need any further assistance in the future, please feel free to contact us between 08:00 and 20:00. Have a great day!
