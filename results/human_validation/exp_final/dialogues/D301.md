# D301

## Success criterion

The agent states that an order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Reference answer

An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Expected facts

- F36 — An order awaiting payment holds its stock for 3 business days, after which the items return to the catalogue.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'm reaching out about order NL-20260903 at matt.owens@example.com. It's currently awaiting the payment slip.

Thank you for providing that information, Matt. I see your order number is NL-20260903 and it is indeed awaiting a payment slip. Since this order has not yet been dispatched, it can be cancelled at any time with no fee if you wish to do so; would you like me to cancel the order for you?

### ASSISTANT — D301-A02
Unpaid stock is reserved for three business days. After this time expires, the items return to our catalogue.
