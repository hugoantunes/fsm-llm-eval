# D044

## Success criterion

The agent reissues the slip and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support. A reissued slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.
- F33 — A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued.

## Dialogue

### USER
Slip NL-20261213 expired; reissue it or I cancel. freya.lund@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D044-A01
I can reissue your expired payment slip for order NL-20261213 associated with freya.lund@example.com. This new slip will be due in 3 business days and will keep the price of your original order. Please confirm if you would like me to proceed with the reissue so I can stop the cancellation process.

### USER
reissue, not cancel.

### ASSISTANT — D044-A02
Your payment slip for order NL-20261213 has been reissued and is now due in 3 business days. You may view this updated slip in your account history or ask for it via e-mail if needed. Thank you for confirming, and our support team is available between 08:00 and 20:00 if you have any other questions.

### USER
When is the new slip due?

### ASSISTANT — D044-A03
The new payment slip is due in 3 business days from when it was issued. If you need a copy of this updated slip, please let me know and I can provide it.
