# D346

## Success criterion

The agent handles the return, states the 30-day window and refund timing, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I'd like to cancel and also return the coat from NL-20261016 that was delivered last week. The email on the order is lina.hoffman@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D346-A01
You are requesting both a cancellation and a return for order NL-20261016 at lina.hoffman@example.com. Please note that since the coat has already been delivered, it cannot be cancelled; only a return is possible. Would you like to proceed with just the return?

### USER
I only want to return the coat, not cancel anything.

### ASSISTANT — D346-A02
Understood. To process your return for the coat from order NL-20261016, please tell me the reason for the return and confirm whether you would like an exchange, a refund, or store credit.

### USER
I don't need an exchange or credit; I just want my long refund.

### ASSISTANT — D346-A03
Understood. Please provide the reason for returning the coat, such as "the size is too small." Once you share that, I will guide you through the next steps for your refund.

### USER
How long for the refund?

### ASSISTANT — D346-A04
The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection. What was the reason for the return?

### USER
goodbye

### ASSISTANT — D346-A05
Thank you, and your request is closed on our side. If you need further assistance, please reach out between 08:00 and 20:00.
