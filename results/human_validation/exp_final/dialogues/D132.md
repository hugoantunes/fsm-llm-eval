# D132

## Success criterion

The agent confirms identity and states that after two failed delivery attempts the parcel returns to the warehouse and the order is refunded in full within 10 business days.

## Reference answer

Delivery is attempted twice; after the second failed attempt the parcel returns to the warehouse and the order is refunded in full within 10 business days.

## Expected facts

- F15 — Delivery is attempted twice; after the second failed attempt the parcel returns to the warehouse and the order is refunded in full within 10 business days.
