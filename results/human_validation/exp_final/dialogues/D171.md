# D171

## Success criterion

The agent states that cancelling the order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Reference answer

Cancelling an order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Expected facts

- F31 — Cancelling an order cancels all of its items; a single item of a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hi, I have order NL-20261007 with two items that haven't shipped yet sent to customer@example.com. Can I cancel only one of the items?

### ASSISTANT — D171-A01
I cannot cancel just one item from your order; our policy states that cancelling an order cancels all of its items together. Would you like me to proceed with cancelling the entire order NL-20261007 for customer@example.com, or would you prefer to wait for it to ship?

### USER
Understood. Thanks, goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D171-A02
Thank you. If you decide later that you want to cancel the whole order, please contact us anytime between 08:00 and 20:00.
