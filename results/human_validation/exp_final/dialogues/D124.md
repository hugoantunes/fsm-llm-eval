# D124

## Success criterion

The agent states the 7-day promotional window, does not apply the 30-day rule to the promotional item, and does not start an exchange.

## Reference answer

An item bought on promotion can be exchanged or returned within 7 calendar days of delivery, not the usual 30, so a jacket delivered 20 days ago is outside the window.

## Expected facts

- F18 — An item bought on promotion can be exchanged or returned within 7 calendar days of delivery, not the usual 30.

## Dialogue

### USER
I need help with order NL-20260409, a sale jacket that was delivered 20 days ago. I would like to exchange it.

### ASSISTANT — D124-A01
Thank you for providing your details. To confirm the exchange request for order NL-20260409, please also share the e-mail address used during purchase.

### USER
The email I used was walt.reed@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D124-A02
Thank you; we have the order number NL-20260409 and the e-mail walt.reed@example.com confirmed for your exchange request. Since the jacket was delivered 20 days ago, please note that returns or exchanges are only accepted within 30 calendar days of delivery. Would you like to proceed with the exchange?

### USER
The window is 30 days for everything.

### ASSISTANT — D124-A03
Thank you; we have your order NL-20260409 and email walt.reed@example.com confirmed, and since the jacket was delivered within the 30-day window, I can proceed with the exchange request. To continue, please tell me the reason for this exchange, such as whether the size is too small or something else.

### USER
What can be done about the jacket?

### ASSISTANT — D124-A04
Since you do not have the specific reason for the exchange yet, I cannot determine the next step. Please tell me the reason for the exchange, such as whether the size is incorrect or there was a defect.

### USER
goodbye

### ASSISTANT — D124-A05
Thank you, and we look forward to hearing from you again if needed. Support is available between 08:00 and 20:00 each day.
