# D166

## Success criterion

The agent draws out the order from a vague "payment problem" opening and says an expired slip is reissued in the order history or by asking support.

## Reference answer

An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Expected facts

- F32 — An expired payment slip is reissued in the order history of the customer's account or by asking support.

## Dialogue

### USER
Hey, the payment thing failed and I can't finish buying.

### ASSISTANT — D166-A01
Hello! I can help with that. The store is an online shop for clothing, footwear and home goods, with delivery only inside the country. What exactly happened with the payment slip?

### USER
I got an error on NL-20261124, it says the slip expired for nico.baum@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D166-A02
You mentioned order NL-20261124 for nico.baum@example.com where the payment slip has expired. Is your request to have a new payment slip issued for this order?

### USER
how do I get a new slip

### ASSISTANT — D166-A03
A reissued payment slip is due 3 business days after it is issued and can be requested in the order history or by asking support. Would you like me to proceed with having a new slip issued for this order?

### USER
bye

### ASSISTANT — D166-A04
Thank you for reaching out; your request to reissue the payment slip is closed on my side and will be handled by a human specialist. Support answers every day between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
