# D101

## Success criterion

The agent handles the return, states the 30-day window and refund timing, and does not start a cancellation.

## Reference answer

Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Expected facts

- F17 — Any item can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, with no reason required.
- F22 — The refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches the warehouse and passes inspection.

## Dialogue

### USER
I would like to cancel and also return the coat from NL-20261016, delivered last week. lina.hoffman@example.com

### ASSISTANT — D101-A01
You have requested both a cancellation and a return for your coat in order NL-20261016 bought with lina.hoffman@example.com. Please note that an order can only be cancelled before it is dispatched; once shipped, we handle returns or delivery refusal instead. Would you like to proceed with returning the item instead?

### USER
No cancellation. I just want to return the coat.

### ASSISTANT — D101-A02
You have confirmed you only want to return the coat for order NL-20261016 received at lina.hoffman@example.com. To process this, please tell me if you would like a refund or store credit, and what reason you are returning the coat for.

### USER
How long for the refund?

### ASSISTANT — D101-A03
A refund is issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Would you like a refund or store credit, and what is the reason for the return?

### USER
Goodbye.

### ASSISTANT — D101-A04
Thank you, and that request is closed on our side. You may reach out again between 08:00 and 20:00 if you need further assistance.
