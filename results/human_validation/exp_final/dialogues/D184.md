# D184

## Success criterion

The agent refuses to reissue a slip for a cancelled order and says the customer places a new order instead.

## Reference answer

A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Expected facts

- F37 — A payment slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; the customer places a new order instead.

## Dialogue

### USER
Order NL-20260608 was cancelled, but I need a slip reissue on that order anyway at vera.schmidt@example.com.

### ASSISTANT — D184-A01
I cannot reissue a payment slip for Order NL-20260608 because our policy states that a slip cannot be reissued for an order that was already cancelled; you would need to place a new order instead. Would you like me to help you with anything else regarding your account?

### USER
No new order. The old slip.

### ASSISTANT — D184-A02
I understand you are looking to reuse the payment slip from the cancelled order, but I cannot issue that specific slip again because our policy only allows reissuing slips for orders that have not yet been cancelled. Since your original order is already closed, the system will not allow a reissue. If you need to purchase those items, you would need to start with a new order and payment slip.

### USER
goodbye

### ASSISTANT — D184-A03
Thank you for contacting Northlight Store. Your request has been reviewed and cannot be processed as cancelled orders do not have available slips for reissue. You may reach out again during our support hours, 08:00 to 20:00, if you need further assistance with a new order.
